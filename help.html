About

Marker Hunter finds the Texas historical markers near you, reads them out loud, and lets each traveler collect stamps, badges, and trophies in their own passport.

Stamping a marker

You earn a stamp by standing near a marker, within about 500 feet of the spot the Texas Historical Commission has on file.

  1. Open the Nearby tab. It shows the closest marker, how far away it is, and a Listen button.
  2. Walk or drive to the marker. In a spot with lots of markers, use ◀ Prev and Next ▶ to pick the one you're at.
  3. Tap Get stamp. Every passport turned on under Who's traveling gets the stamp.

If the button is gray, it tells you why:

  • Get closer · 420 ft: you're not close enough yet.
  • GPS is fuzzy · tap to sharpen: your phone isn't sure where you are. Tap the button, or step away from buildings and trees, and it checks again.

If the vehicle is moving over about 10 mph, the app asks Are you a passenger? before stamping. Drivers should leave the screen alone; driving mode reads markers out loud.

A green ✓ Already stamped tag means this marker is already in your passport.

Driving mode

Driving mode announces markers ahead of you out loud, so nobody has to look at the screen. Keep the app open on the Nearby tab; the screen stays on while it runs.

  1. On the Nearby tab, tap Start driving mode.
  2. Drive. When a marker is coming up, the app says its name and reads it.
  3. Tap Stop when you're done.

In a town with lots of markers close together, the app says Historic area ahead once a day and then stays quiet. The closest marker stays on screen; use Prev and Next to browse them. Tap 🚩 Mark if something odd happens, and the moment is saved for troubleshooting.

Your passport and Lone Stars

Every stamp, badge, and trophy earns Lone Stars, but you collect them by hand in your passport booklet. A red number on the Passport tab means stars are waiting.

  1. Open the Passport tab and tap 📖 Open booklet.
  2. Flip through the pages. Marker stamps are grouped by county, A to Z.
  3. Tap Collect ★ at the bottom of a page to add its stars to your bank. On badge and trophy pages, tap anything showing +3 ★.

Waiting stars never expire. When the booklet gets long, tap ☰ Contents to jump to a county.

You earnLone Stars
A marker, museum, or courthouse stamp1
A badge3
Each trophy level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Titanium)5

Spend your stars on the Passport tab and in the booklet:

  • Reveal a hidden badge or trophy to see how to earn it (2 to 5 stars, by rarity). Common ones always show.
  • Unlock a county's total to see how many markers it has (2 stars). The progress bar works without it.
  • Save a streak after a missed week (5 stars).
  • Passport covers: colors (2), awareness ribbons (1), and Texas designs (5). Tap 🎨 Change cover on the booklet's front.

Lone Stars can't be bought. They're earned only by exploring.

Courthouses and counties

Any stamp in a county counts as a visit; stamping that county's courthouse collects it. Texas has 254 counties to collect.

  • Visited counties are listed on the Places I Visited page of your booklet and stay there. A ✓ means you've also collected it.
  • Collected counties get a county stamp in the booklet, sorted A to Z.
  • Courthouses show as red pins on the Map tab. Tap one, go to it, and tap Collect.
  • Many courthouses also have a historical marker out front. Stamping either one gives you both: the marker stamp and the county.

A passport for each traveler

Each person can have their own passport on one phone, with their own stamps, stars, and cover. Up to 8 passports fit on a phone.

  1. Go to Settings, then Who's traveling.
  2. Tap + Add a passport and type a name. Tap a name later to rename it.
  3. Switch on everyone who's in the car today. When you tap Get stamp, every passport that's switched on gets the stamp.

On the Passport tab, tap a name at the top to see that person's progress and booklet. Each traveler collects their own Lone Stars. Tap 🌼 next to a name to set a spring break week for the Spring Seeker badge.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
"Location is off"Tap Open Settings, then Location, choose While Using the App, and turn on Precise Location.
"Can't find you yet"Step outside or away from tall buildings, then tap Try again.
Can't stamp while parked right at a markerTap the gray button to sharpen your GPS. Some markers' recorded spots are a little off; get as close as you can.
No soundTurn the volume up. Check that Read aloud automatically isn't set to Off.
Choppy voice in the carTry a Standard voice under Narrator voice, or unplug and reconnect CarPlay.
Driving mode stopped talkingKeep the app open on screen; announcements don't play with the phone locked yet.
Report opens the wrong email appThe first time you send a report, pick Gmail, Outlook, or your default email app. To pick again, go to Settings, Help, Email app for reports.
Something else isn't rightIn Settings, tap Share report under Diagnostics and email it to support@markerhunterapp.com.

Contact us

Found a problem, have an idea, or spotted a marker that's wrong? Email support@markerhunterapp.com. We read every message.

To help us fix a problem faster:

  1. Tell us your phone model and what you were doing when it happened.
  2. Attach a diagnostics report: in Settings, tap Share report under Diagnostics, then choose Mail. It stays on your phone until you send it.
  3. A screenshot helps too: press the side button and volume up at the same time.

If a marker's text or location is wrong, tap Report a problem with this marker on that marker. It goes straight to the Texas Historical Commission, which keeps the official records.
